The election is over.

The November 3, 2026 election has ended, and this guide has closed. Thank you to every Wisconsin voter who used it to walk into the booth prepared.

My Wisconsin Ballot was a nonpartisan pilot: your races and your candidates, side by side, built from official sources — and nothing else. It never endorsed, ranked, or recommended a candidate.

Looking for results?

Results are the officials' job, so that's where we send you. Wisconsin posts official results county by county — the Wisconsin Elections Commission's MyVote site explains how results are reported and links all 72 county clerks.
